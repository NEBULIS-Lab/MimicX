From human video to robot execution

MimicX turns a human demonstration into a humanoid skill, then uses the robot's execution to refine its supervision. Motion reconstruction and retargeting establish a reference; rollout feedback guides what the policy learns next.

Execution becomes supervision

A registered motion reference establishes the task. Policy feedback determines how that task is practiced and rewarded.

One refinement pass

  1. Read the rollout

    Localize where execution departs from the demonstration.

    When
    Failure windows
    Where
    Root, limbs and contacts
    What
    Pose and dynamics errors

  2. Change the supervision

    Coordinate task-aware tracking objectives and targeted practice; continue from the current policy.

  3. Keep verified progress

    Repeated execution decides the update. Completion, failure horizon and tracking quality take priority over reward.

The selected policy starts the next pass.

Tracking quality and execution

Direct comparisons evaluate learned policies on the same demonstrated motion. Controlled continuation and collision-scene experiments examine how refinement changes execution.

Faster feedback. The same decision.

MimicX-HLoop overlaps GPU rollout and CPU diagnosis along a dependency graph. Selection uses the complete evaluation evidence, regardless of execution order.
